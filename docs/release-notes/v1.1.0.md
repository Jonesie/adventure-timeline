## Map notes, GPX import and a map picker for incidents

### Map notes (new)

Pin a short note to a place on a trip: "road washed out", "lots of potholes", "gate locked after 5".

- **Add one** from a leg on the Legs tab, or with the pin button on the full-screen map. Place it by tapping the map or at your location. Notes are up to 140 characters, with an optional kind (hazard, road condition, fuel, food, view).
- **They show as amber pins** on the trip's map, and pop up during **Play trip** when the vehicle passes them.
- **They can go into what you share:** the post text, the map picture, the PDF and the GPX/KML file. Each note has an "Include when sharing" switch, and notes inside your hidden home area are always left out.
- **They travel with the trip:** GPX notes import back in, they are part of a backup, and the trip data export lists them in route order.

### GPX import (new)

When adding a trip, pick a `.gpx` file from a GPS unit, Strava, Komoot or this app's own GPX export. Its track becomes the trip's route, and its name and dates fill in the title and dates.

- **Each track becomes legs**, split wherever you stopped for five minutes or more (fuel, lunch, overnight). The transport is guessed from the file or from how fast it moved; edit it on the leg if it guessed wrong.
- It is read on the phone and never uploaded.

### Incidents

- **Choose where it happened on a map** when adding an incident, instead of only using your current location.
- **A better place when you don't tag one:** the incident is placed where the trip's legs say you were at that time.

### Good to know

- **The database upgrades itself** the first time you open the app. Nothing is removed. It's still a good idea to make a backup first (Settings → Back up diary).
- No new permissions are asked for, and nothing leaves your phone unless you share it.
