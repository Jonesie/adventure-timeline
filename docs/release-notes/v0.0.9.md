## Richer posts, GPX/KML export, and removing companions

### Sharing a trip

- **Posts are better at staying in one piece.** The post text is now sent as a picture (title, trip facts, the map and your incidents highlighted), so Messenger and Facebook keep it. Your photos follow, then a closing card with the app icon, the link and a QR code.
- **Each leg and incidents have their own switches** on the Share tab, and the settings are grouped under **Words** and **Trip facts**. A read-only preview shows what will be sent. The footer is always added, with a smaller one on PDFs and a QR code on pictures and PDFs.
- **GPX and KML export** are new formats on the Share tab: the recorded route with timestamps, the places of each leg and, if you like, your incidents as waypoints.
- **Your Share choices are remembered.** Dates, star rating, companions, route summary, each leg and incidents keep their setting from one trip to the next. The message, title, photos, trip notes and hide-home are deliberately not remembered.
- **Hide my home area** now also applies to the live map, the start and end dots, the trip data export and the place names in a post's leg list.

### Companions

- **You can now remove a companion.** Tap the **x** on a companion's chip in a trip to take them off that trip.
- **Settings → People → Companions** lists everyone with how many trips and incidents they are on, and lets you delete a companion (after a confirmation). The trips and incidents themselves are kept.

### One note per trip

Trips used to have both a note and "impressions". They are now a single **note**. Anything you had written as impressions is folded into the trip's note, including when you restore an older backup.

### Maps

- **The Waypoints and Photos switches on a trip's map are remembered**, like on the all-trips map.
- **Fixed: the all-trips map switches didn't really stick.** They looked right on screen but were forgotten when you left the app. They now survive a restart.

### Fixes

- **Fixed: a photo taken in the app could be lost** if Android reclaimed the app while the camera was open.
- **Fixed: rotating the phone** no longer clears what you were typing, closes dialogs or drops a voice recording in progress.

### Settings

- **Share app** opens your phone's share sheet with a link to the Play Store listing.
- **Send feedback** starts an email to us with the app and Android versions filled in.

### Good to know

- **There is a database change in this release** (the notes merge above). It is automatic, but **Settings → Back up diary** first if you want a safety net.
- Nothing new is asked of you in permissions, and nothing leaves your phone unless you share it.
