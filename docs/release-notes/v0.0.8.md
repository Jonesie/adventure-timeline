## Share as a post, delete legs, and tidier tablets

### Share a trip as a post (new)

On a trip's **Share** tab there is now a **Post** format, and it is the default. Instead of one long picture you get:

- **A written caption** built from the trip: its title, dates, how many days, how far, the kinds of transport, a star rating and the route (Christchurch → Hanmer Springs → …), then your own message, companions, trip notes and impressions, each only if its switch is on. You can edit the text before sharing.
- **The map and each chosen photo as separate pictures,** so they post as an album. Photos are re-saved for sharing, which strips their embedded location, and the home area can still be hidden from the map.
- **Facebook and Instagram ignore text that comes from another app,** so the caption is also copied to your clipboard when you share. Paste it into the post. WhatsApp, messages, email and most other apps take the text directly.
- **Picture** and **PDF** are still there.

### A footer on everything you share

Posts, pictures and PDFs now end with "Made with Adventure Timeline" and a link to the website, with the app icon on a picture or PDF. A switch on the Share tab turns it off.

### Share tab

- **The map can be collapsed** with the arrow above it, to leave room for the caption and photos. It is still shared as you framed it.
- **Export trip data has moved.** The zip for video editing now has its own page, **Export trip data…** in the trip's ⋮ menu, so it is no longer mixed in with sharing to people.

### Delete legs

Tap a leg and choose **Delete this leg** (or **Delete these N legs** for a grouped row). It removes the leg and the route recorded during it, so the map has a gap there. Photos, notes and incidents stay on the trip. If you delete a trip's last leg, its whole route goes but the trip stays. Re-importing the same Timeline file brings deleted legs back.

### Bigger screens

- **Lists and forms no longer stretch across a wide screen.** On tablets they are held to a readable width and centred. Phones look the same as before.
- The welcome screen's buttons now respect their intended width as well.

### Good to know

- No database changes in this release. As always, **Settings → Back up diary** first if you want a safety net.
- Nothing new is asked of you in permissions, and nothing leaves your phone unless you share it.
