## Export a trip for video editing, fuller backups and a lighter dark map

_v0.0.5 was not published to Google Play, so this release includes everything from it._

### Export a trip as a zip (new)

On a trip's **Share** tab, under **Trip data for a video**, **Export trip data (zip)** packs the trip into one file you can send to Drive or your computer, ready to use alongside other photos and clips in a video editor.

- **`trip.json`** holds the trip (title, dates with time zone, rating, notes, companions), every leg (transport, where it started and ended with names and coordinates, times, duration, distance, difficulty, enjoyment), the full route as `[lat, lon, time]` points, the stops between legs, your notes, your incidents (type, severity, reason, who was involved) and a list of the media with where and when each one was taken.
- **Place names** for photos, notes and incidents are looked up from their positions (this needs a network connection). A photo with no GPS fix gets the position of the trip's route at that moment, marked `"locationSource": "route"`.
- **Incidents have their own location,** separate from any video attached to them (a clip can run well before and after the moment itself). If an incident has no saved position it takes where the route was at that time. Incidents are numbered, and each media entry for an incident carries its `incidentNumber`.
- **`media/`** holds the photos, named in the order they were taken (`001_20261023-105332.jpg`) and dated with when they were taken, so they sort into place in an editor. Videos and voice notes are optional switches, off by default because videos can be large.
- **`route-map.png`** is a picture of the route map, if the map is switched on.

### Backups

- **The incident reasons list is now in the backup,** so restoring on a new phone brings back your own list. Backups made before this release leave the phone's list alone.
- The Settings and backup dialog text now says incidents are included (they always were).

### Dark theme map

- The dark map is **lighter and blue-grey** instead of near-black purple, and place names, roads and routes are **brighter** so they are easier to read.

### Good to know

- No database changes in this release. As always, **Settings → Back up diary** first if you want a safety net.
- Nothing new is asked of you in permissions, and nothing leaves your phone unless you share it.
