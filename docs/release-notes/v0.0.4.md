## Incidents, a new icon and a welcome screen

This is the biggest update so far. The headline is **incident logging**: a place to record the spills, wrong turns and river crossings gone sideways, with photos and videos, even before the trip exists.

### Incidents (new)

- **Log what went wrong.** There is a new **Incidents** tab next to Map and Trips, and a "Log an incident…" item in each trip's menu.
- **What happened:** pick from a dropdown of 17 types: fell off, off the road, crash or collision, near miss, river or water mishap, stuck, lost, breakdown, flat tyre, out of fuel, injury, bad weather, road closed, gear failure, wildlife, delay, other.
- **How bad:** Minor, Moderate or Serious (optional; tap again to clear).
- **Who was involved:** you, one or more companions, or anyone new. Companions named on an incident are added to the trip too.
- **Reason (optional):** "Not my fault", "Situation beyond my capability", "Lack of focus", "Stupidity", "Rather not say", "Other", or blank. The list is yours to change in **Settings → Incident reasons**: rename (existing incidents follow), remove, add your own, or restore the originals.
- **When, and which trip:** the date and time are at the top of the form. The **Trip** choice is optional. Leave it blank and the incident joins whichever trip covers that date once the trip exists. If you pick a trip, the date must fall inside the trip's dates, or Save stays disabled.
- **Works before the trip exists.** Log it on the road. When the trip is imported from Google Timeline, the incident attaches itself, with its photos and videos, and takes its place on the map from the trip's route if it was logged without a location. "Tag with my current location" pins an incident you log right after it happens.
- **Photos and videos:** take a photo, record a video, or add them from your phone, straight from the incident's card. Open one full screen and tap the broken-link icon to **detach** it from the incident (it stays in the trip's media). An incident with no trip yet has nowhere to keep a detached file, so that one is removed instead, and the app says so first.
- **See them everywhere:** red pins on a trip's map and on the all-trips map (with an Incidents toggle), a warning icon on trip tiles that have incidents, an Incidents tab inside each trip, an Incidents tile and breakdown in Stats, and ⚠ lines in "Have I been here before?".
- **Backups include incidents**, who was involved, and their photos and videos. Older backups still restore.

### Looks and first run

- **New app icon,** using the same artwork as the splash screen.
- **Welcome screen.** An empty diary now shows a short guide with buttons to import from Google Timeline, add a trip, log an incident or restore a backup, and a link to the full help page. It goes away as soon as you add a trip or an incident.
- **About** now links to the website and user guide, and mentions incidents.

### Good to know

- The database upgrades itself on first launch and keeps your trips, photos and notes. As always, **Settings → Back up diary** first if you want a safety net.
- Incident logging uses the permissions the app already had (location, camera). Nothing new is asked for, and nothing leaves your phone.
- Not in this release: detecting a sudden shock and prompting you to log an incident, and a live recording mode. Both are planned.
