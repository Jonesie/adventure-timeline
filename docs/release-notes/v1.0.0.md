## Play trip: watch your trip back, with sound

### Play trip (new)

Tap the **▶ button** on a trip's map, or on the full-screen map, to play the whole trip back as an animation.

- **The route draws itself** with a vehicle travelling along it: on foot, bike, motorbike, car, bus, train, plane or boat.
- **Photos pop up with their captions** where they were taken, and **incidents stop the show** with a sound.
- **Nights and untracked gaps are skipped**, so the story keeps moving. A flight with no recorded trail is drawn as a dashed straight line.
- **Pause, scrub, 1x / 2x / 4x speed and mute** controls are along the bottom. Photos and incidents stay on screen for the same time at any speed.

### Sounds

- **Each leg has its own quiet sound while it travels:** footsteps, bike, motorbike, car, 4WD, bus, train, plane or boat. Boats add a fog horn and a ship's bell now and then. Everything goes quiet at a stop, on pause and when muted.
- **Incidents have their own sounds:** a crash, a tyre screech, a spluttering engine, thunder, a scream for a fall, a scream cut off by a splash for a water mishap, and a siren for an injury.
- **A serious incident also gets a siren** after its own sound.
- **Screams have two takes,** picked at random and never the same one twice in a row.
- **When the trip ends, a random celebration plays,** never the same twice in a row: an orchestral ta-dah, a drum roll and cymbal, a harp run into an orchestral chord, or a crowd clapping and cheering.

### Good to know

- **Sound only plays while you are watching a trip.** Turn the speaker off with the mute button, or just lower your phone's volume.
- Nothing new is asked of you in permissions, there is no database change, and nothing leaves your phone unless you share it.
