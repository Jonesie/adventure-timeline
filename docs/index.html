Your private travel diary

Bring in the trips Google Maps Timeline already found, then add the photos, ratings and stories that make each one memorable. Everything stays on your phone.

Download for Android Google Play: coming soon

Free and open source. No account, no ads, no tracking. Needs Android 8.0 or newer.

What it does

A diary for the trips you take: where you went, how you got there, who with, and what you saw.

🧭

Starts with your Timeline

Import the trips Google Maps Timeline has already detected, with their legs, transport and route. Re-import later and your own edits are kept.

🗺️

Route maps that tell the story

See every trip on an OpenStreetMap route. Legs are coloured by how hard they were, and the home screen shows all your trips on one map, coloured by star rating.

📸

Photos, video and voice notes

Capture them in the app, or link the ones already on your phone. Photos taken along your route are suggested for the trip, and they show up as pins on the map.

⭐

Rate and remember

Give each trip a star rating, and each leg a difficulty and an enjoyment score. Quick timestamped notes capture how it felt at the time.

⚠️

Log what goes wrong

Tipped over in a river? Took a wrong turn? Log it on the spot, with photos and video. It finds its trip once that is imported, and shows up on the map.

📤

Share a trip

Turn a trip into a high-resolution picture or a PDF with the map, your notes and your best photos. Your home area can be left off the map.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your diary lives on your phone, and a one-tap backup zip goes wherever you choose, such as Drive or your computer.

A look inside

A companion's name is blurred. The photos and videos are real ones from the trip.

How to use it

Five minutes to your first trip.

Install the app

Google Play is coming soon. Until then, open the latest release on your phone, download app-release.apk and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time.

Add your first trips

Option A: import from Google Maps Timeline. Timeline lives on your phone now, so export it from there:

  1. On the phone, go to Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline → Export Timeline data. This saves a Timeline.json file.
  2. In Adventure Timeline, open the Trips tab and tap the cloud-download icon.
  3. Choose the Timeline.json file, tick the trips you want and import them. A couple of years of data is large, so give it a moment.

Option B: start from scratch. On the Trips tab, tap + to add a trip with a title, dates, notes and the people you travelled with.

Open a trip

Tap any trip. It has six tabs:

  • Map: dates, star rating, route map and companions. Tap the full-screen button for a bigger map, and switch on Waypoints to see each stop.
  • Legs: each stretch of the journey by walk, bike, motorbike, car, 4WD, bus, train, plane or boat. Rate its difficulty (1 to 5) and how much you enjoyed it.
  • Media: your photos, videos and voice notes in one gallery.
  • Notes: quick timestamped impressions, tagged with where you were.
  • Incidents: anything that went wrong on this trip, with its photos and videos.
  • Share: build a picture or PDF of the trip.

Add photos, video and audio

  • Use the camera and microphone buttons to capture something new.
  • Choose Add from phone. The picker opens on the trip's dates, and the photos are linked, not copied, so they take no extra space.
  • Try Near route to find photos taken within 1, 2 or 5 km of the trail, and look at the suggestions on the Media tab.
  • The auto-add button pulls in everything your camera shot during the trip's dates.

Log an incident

  • Tap the Incidents tab at the bottom, then +. You don't need a trip: log it right there on the road.
  • Choose what happened from the dropdown (fell off, ran off the road, river mishap, got lost, breakdown, flat tyre, injury, bad weather and more) and how bad it was. Say who was involved: you, one or more companions, or add a new name. If you want, pick a reason from the dropdown (leave it blank if you would rather not say, and change the list in Settings). Add a few words if you like.
  • Leave "Tag with my current location" ticked and it is pinned where you are. Logging later? Change the date and time.
  • On the incident's card, tap the camera, video or gallery button to attach photos and videos. Open one and tap the broken-link icon to detach it from the incident.
  • Once the trip is imported from Google Timeline, the incident joins the trip that covers its time, and appears on the map.

Explore your travels

  • The home screen is a map of all your trips, coloured by star rating. Tap a route to open the trip, and use the chips to filter by transport.
  • The Trips tab lists them. Sort by date or rating, or search by title, place name or near where you are now.
  • The stats icon shows totals, distance by transport and year, and your highlights.
  • Tap the compass icon to ask, "Have I been here before?" It lists earlier notes and photos within about 1 km.

Share a trip

On the Share tab, pick a title and caption, choose up to 7 photos, decide whether to include companions and notes, then share it as a picture or a PDF through your phone's share sheet. Photos are re-saved for sharing, which strips their embedded location.

Back it up

Open Settings → Backup and tap Back up diary. It saves every trip, route, note and attached file to one zip. Put it on Drive or your computer. Restore from backup brings it all back on a new phone (it replaces what's in the app). Turn on the Backup reminder and the app will nudge you every 7, 14 or 30 days.

Make it yours

In Settings you can pick a light or dark theme, set your Home area so photos taken at home aren't suggested for every trip, link companions to people in your contacts, and free up space by swapping duplicate imported copies for links to the originals.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, in the app's private storage. It is only copied somewhere else when you make a backup or share a trip, and those go where you send them.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It only uses the internet to fetch map tiles from OpenStreetMap and to look up place names with Android's built-in geocoder.

Does it track my location?

No. It never records your location in the background. It only reads your location when you use a feature that needs it, such as tagging a note or centring the map on you.

Why does it ask for permissions?

Only when you use the feature that needs them: camera and microphone to capture media, photos and videos to attach them, location for notes and "near me", and contacts if you want to link companions. You can refuse any of them and still use the rest. The privacy policy explains each one.

Is it affiliated with Google?

No. Adventure Timeline is an independent project. "Google" and "Google Maps Timeline" are trademarks of Google LLC, used only to describe the files the app can import.

Is it free?

Yes, and it is open source under the GNU General Public License v3.0. You can read the code and build it yourself.
