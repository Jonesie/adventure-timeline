## App name (30)

Adventure Timeline: Trip Diary

## Short description (80)

Trip diary and travel journal: routes, photos, notes, ratings. Offline, private.

## Full description

Capture your adventures. Adventure Timeline is a private travel diary and trip log that turns the journeys
you've already taken into a journal you'll want to keep: the route, the photos, who you were with and how it
felt.

Perfect for road trips, motorbike and bike tours, hikes, 4WD adventures, train and plane journeys and every
other way of getting from A to B.

IMPORT YOUR TRIPS, THEN MAKE THEM YOURS
Bring in the trips Google Maps Timeline has already detected, with their legs, transport and route. Then add
what Timeline can't: star ratings, photos, voice notes and the story of the day. Re-import any time; your own
edits are kept.

TRIP LOG AND ROUTE MAP
• Every trip has dates, notes, companions and a 1-5 star rating
• See the route on an OpenStreetMap map, with each leg coloured by how hard it was
• Log each leg by transport: walk, bike, motorbike, car, 4WD, bus, train, plane or boat
• Rate the difficulty and the enjoyment of every leg
• One all-trips map shows everywhere you've been, coloured by rating

PHOTOS, VIDEO AND VOICE NOTES
• Shoot photos and record voice notes inside a trip, or link the ones already on your phone
• Photos are pinned on the map where they were taken
• Find the photos taken along a route, and leave out the ones from around home

INCIDENT REPORTING
Log what went wrong the moment it happens: a fall, a breakdown, a flat tyre, a wrong turn, bad weather,
an injury. Record how bad it was, who was involved and why, with notes and photos. Incidents appear on your
maps and in your stats, so the lessons stay with the trip.

SHARE YOUR JOURNEY
Compose a trip post with the route map, a caption, your incidents and your best photos, then share it through
any app. Or export a single picture, a PDF, or the route as a GPX or KML file. You can hide your home area from
shared maps.

SEARCH, STATS AND MEMORIES
• Search your diary by title, place name, or near where you are now
• Trip stats: totals, distance by transport and by year, and your highlights
• "Have I been here before?" lists your earlier notes and photos from the same spot

PRIVATE AND WORKS OFFLINE
• Your diary lives on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking
• Nothing is synced or uploaded; the only network use is map tiles and place-name lookups
• Back up the whole diary to one file and restore it on a new phone
• Light, dark or follow-the-system theme
• Free and open source, so you can check for yourself

HOW IT WORKS
1. Export your Timeline on your phone (Settings > Location > Location services > Timeline > Export Timeline data) and import it, or add a trip by hand.
2. Open a trip to see its route, legs, photos, notes and sharing options.
3. Add your pictures, rate the legs and write a few lines while the memories are fresh.
